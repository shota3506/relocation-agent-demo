# Moving Cost Estimator Skill

This skill provides expert calculation rules, standard market multipliers, and negotiation strategies for upfront rental moving expenses.

---

## 1. Upfront Cost Standard Benchmark (~4.5x - 5.0x Monthly Rent)

| Expense Item | Standard Rate | Notes & Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Security Deposit** | 1.0 month rent | Added +1.0 month if tenant has pets. Refundable minus cleaning. |
| **Key Money (Reikin)** | 1.0 month rent | Non-refundable fee to landlord. Negotiable on stale listings. |
| **Agency Brokerage Fee** | 1.0 month rent + 10% tax (1.10x) | Standard agency rate. Discountable (0.5x - 0x) at select brokerages. |
| **Advance First Month Rent** | 1.0 month rent + management fee | Prorated daily if moving mid-month. |
| **Guarantor Company Fee** | ~50% of 1 month total payment | Initial fee (~50% monthly cost). Annual renewal fee applies. |
| **Fire / Renters Insurance** | ~¥20,000 / $150 | 2-year standard mandatory policy. |
| **Key Replacement Fee** | ~¥22,000 / $150 | Standard cylinder or dimple lock replacement. |

---

## 2. Negotiation & Cost-Saving Playbook

1. **Key Money Removal**: Target properties that have been on the market for $>30$ days. Landlords often agree to drop key money to secure immediate occupancy.
2. **Free Rent Promotion**: Request 0.5 to 1.0 month of free rent to offset the advance rent obligation.
3. **Brokerage Commission Discount**: Many consumer-facing brokerages offer flat 50% commission reductions.
